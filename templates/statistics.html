📘 About ELO Rating System

The ELO rating system is a method for calculating the relative skill levels of players. Players start at a rating of 1500. When you win against a higher-rated opponent, you gain more points. When you lose against a lower-rated opponent, you lose more points.

🧮 How It Works

Step 1: Calculate Expected Score

E = 1 / (1 + 10(opponent_rating - your_rating) / 400)

This gives the probability (0-1) that you will win based on rating difference.

Step 2: Update Rating After Game

Rnew = Rold + K × (Actual - Expected)

K=32 determines rating volatility. Actual is 1 for win, 0 for loss.

⚙️ System Parameters

K-Factor 32 Rating change per game
Starting Rating 1500 Default for new players
Win Score 1.0 Actual score for winner
Loss Score 0.0 Actual score for loser
